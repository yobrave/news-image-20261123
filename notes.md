## Main stories (the three leads)

1. **CLIMATE / EUROPE — COP31, the longest UN climate talks ever, ends with no deal.**
   The two-week summit in Antalya broke down two days after it was due to end —
   "the longest-running in the summit's history" — after the EU rejected a final
   text that omitted all mention of fossil fuels. COP31 president Murat Kurum
   conceded the deal was "not adopted"; European nations, small island states and
   activists blamed oil-producing countries. Australia's prime minister, whose
   country co-hosted the summit, attributed the failure to "difficult
   geopolitical times". AP's analysis calls the collapse "years in the making"
   and warns it matters "as extreme weather worsens". This was the single most
   prominent story across all five snapshots.
   Sources: BBC ("Longest-ever UN climate talks end with no deal as deep
   divisions exposed"; "What's next after no deal at COP31 climate talks?");
